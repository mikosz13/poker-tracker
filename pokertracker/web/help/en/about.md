# About

**Poker Tracker {{version}}**: a free tournament tracker for GGPoker. It reads the files PokerCraft gives you and answers the questions a recreational player asks: am I winning, how much of it is luck, where do I lose chips.

## How to report a bug {#bug}

Open an issue on [GitHub (Issues)](https://github.com/mikosz13/poker-tracker/issues/new) and write:

- the app's version (at the top of this page) and your system (macOS or Windows, which version);
- what you did, what happened and what you expected;
- a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Do not attach whole PokerCraft exports: they are your hands. For one hand use **Copy the hand** in the replayer; the text shows opponents by position only.
