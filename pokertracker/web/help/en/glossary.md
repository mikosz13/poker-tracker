# Glossary

- **All-in EV**: What an all-in is worth with your equity when the money went in; luck = actual result − EV.
- **Bounty / KO**: Money for knocking a player out.
- **Flight / Day 2**: Day-1 tournaments of a multi-day event; the stacks that go through meet on Day 2.
- **Late reg / re-entry**: Joining after the start / buying in again after busting.
- **Limp**: Calling the big blind first in.
- **Leak**: A spot that costs you money beyond chance.
- **n**: How many chances a rate rests on.
- **q**: The chance a finding is a false alarm after the correction for every spot.
- **Showdown**: Cards shown at the end of a hand.
- **Spot**: A recurring situation, e.g. defending the big blind against an open.
- **Squeeze**: A re-raise after a raise and a call.
